MM

Phone a restaurant to book dinner, then reconfirm it

A Meta Muse user dictated date, time and party size; Muse called the restaurant, booked the table and shared a transcript. When the booking didn't show online, Muse called back and the host confirmed it.

Source: X (@Stepmark_Jay) · Nov 13, 2026

Copy the prompt
Call [RESTAURANT] and book a table for [N] people on [DATE] at [TIME] under the name [NAME]. Mention it's a [OCCASION]. Ask for a confirmation name or number, and send me the call transcript. Two days before, call again to confirm the booking is still on.

Read a year of card charges and cut $53 a month

A personal AI assistant went through a year of card statements, found a duplicate DashPass subscription, and negotiated an internet bill down in the provider's support chat while the owner approved each step. It also miscounted declined charges, which the author corrected.

Source: Krishan's Tech Blog · Nov 10, 2026

Copy the prompt
Read my card transactions from the last 12 months (posted charges only, ignore declined ones). List every recurring charge with amount, start date and whether it changed price. Flag duplicates and price increases, then suggest which to cancel or negotiate. Don't cancel or change anything without asking me.

GB

Run an inbox at zero with two daily digests

A builder set up scheduled Grok Bot routines with Gmail and Calendar connectors: action threads stay, everything else is labelled and archived, and replies are saved as drafts only. He estimates 1.3 to 1.9 hours saved a week.

Source: emilingemarkarlsson.com · Nov 2, 2026

Copy the prompt
Twice a day, go through my new Gmail. Keep only threads that need a reply or a payment in the inbox; label and archive newsletters, receipts and updates. Never send, delete or trash anything. Then send me a short digest: what needs me, what you filed, and my calendar for the next two weeks.

AA

Give an AI 'inbox chief of staff' strict rules

A small-business owner had an AI assistant triage email through six ordered questions, propose meeting times and draft routine replies, with hard rules: never send, never delete, never commit to prices or dates.

Source: AI Elevation · Oct 28, 2026

Copy the prompt
Triage my inbox. For each email, stop at the first yes: is it noise (archive), already handled (archive), urgent today (label @Urgent), a meeting request (suggest 3 times from my calendar), or a routine reply with known facts (draft it)? Everything else: label @Action Required with a one-line summary. Never send, delete, or agree to any price or date.

Learn the hard way: an AI security audit missed a committed token

A developer asked Claude Code to audit a repo for secrets before going public. It missed a Contentful management token in .claude/mcp.json, a file Claude Code had created, which had been committed into git history.

Source: FieldNotes AI · Mar 7, 2026

List every credential with file, commit and type, then tell me which to rotate and what to add to .gitignore.
