How we rank (ranking v1, updated Nov 15, 2026)

Ranks are Milo's editorial judgment (v1, Nov 15, 2026), based on each tool's official documentation, product pages and public pricing pages, all checked on that date. Each tool is scored on four questions: (1) Does it finish the job end to end for a non-expert? (2) How good and reliable is the result? (3) How cheap is it to get started? (4) How mature and widely used is it? Hands-on tests and reader reports will be added weekly, and ranks will move when they show something new. Sponsors cannot buy or change a rank. Pricing tier is shown only when we confirmed it on the tool's official pricing page; 'freemium' means a free plan plus paid plans, 'paid' means no free plan (a trial or starter credits may exist).

Use cases for this job

GB

Run an inbox at zero with two daily digests

A builder set up scheduled Grok Bot routines with Gmail and Calendar connectors: action threads stay, everything else is labelled and archived, and replies are saved as drafts only. He estimates 1.3 to 1.9 hours saved a week.

Source: emilingemarkarlsson.com · Nov 2, 2026

Copy the prompt
Twice a day, go through my new Gmail. Keep only threads that need a reply or a payment in the inbox; label and archive newsletters, receipts and updates. Never send, delete or trash anything. Then send me a short digest: what needs me, what you filed, and my calendar for the next two weeks.

AA

Give an AI 'inbox chief of staff' strict rules

A small-business owner had an AI assistant triage email through six ordered questions, propose meeting times and draft routine replies, with hard rules: never send, never delete, never commit to prices or dates.

Source: AI Elevation · Oct 28, 2026

Copy the prompt
Triage my inbox. For each email, stop at the first yes: is it noise (archive), already handled (archive), urgent today (label @Urgent), a meeting request (suggest 3 times from my calendar), or a routine reply with known facts (draft it)? Everything else: label @Action Required with a one-line summary. Never send, delete, or agree to any price or date.
